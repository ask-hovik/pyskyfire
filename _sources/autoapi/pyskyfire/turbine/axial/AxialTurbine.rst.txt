pyskyfire.turbine.axial.AxialTurbine
====================================

.. py:class:: pyskyfire.turbine.axial.AxialTurbine(duty, efficiency, stages, *, mean_diameter=None, blade_jet_ratio=None, work_split=None, inlet_swirl=0.0, loss_split=None, rows=None, rotation=COUNTERCLOCKWISE)

   
   Design an axial turbine at mean radius. See the module docstring.


   :Parameters:

       **duty** : :obj:`TurbineDuty`
           What the turbine is asked to do.

       **efficiency** : :class:`python:float`
           Actual work over ``duty.isentropic_head``, on the duty's own basis.

       **stages** : :term:`python:sequence` :obj:`of` :obj:`Stage`
           Stator-rotor pairs, in flow order.

       **mean_diameter** : :class:`python:float`, :obj:`optional`
           Blade mean diameter [m].

       **blade_jet_ratio** : :class:`python:float`, :obj:`optional`
           ``u/c0``, used instead of ``mean_diameter``.

       **work_split** : :term:`python:sequence` :obj:`of` :class:`python:float`, :obj:`optional`
           Each stage's share of the work. Default: equal.

       **inlet_swirl** : :class:`python:float`
           Absolute swirl entering the first stator [m s⁻¹].

       **loss_split** : :term:`python:sequence` :obj:`of` :class:`python:float`, :obj:`optional`
           Each row's share of the entropy rise. Default: equal.

       **rows** : :obj:`RowSettings`
           How the triangles become blades.

       **rotation** : :class:`python:str`
           Which way the rotor turns about +x.
