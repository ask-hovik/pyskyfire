pyskyfire.regen.coupled_solver.FilmHeatFluxModel
================================================

.. py:class:: pyskyfire.regen.coupled_solver.FilmHeatFluxModel(liquid_results: pyskyfire.regen.film_solver.LiquidFilmResults, gaseous_results: Optional[pyskyfire.regen.film_solver.GaseousFilmResults], x_injection: float, h_liquid_wall: Union[float, Callable[[float], float]] = DEFAULT_H_LIQUID_WALL)

   
   Turn a Grisson film solution into a hot-side boundary condition.


   :Parameters:

       **liquid_results** : :obj:`LiquidFilmResults`
           Output of the liquid-film march.

       **gaseous_results** : :obj:`GaseousFilmResults` or :data:`python:None`
           Output of the gaseous-film march, empty/``None`` when the liquid film
           never dries out inside the domain.

       **x_injection** : :class:`python:float`
           Axial station where the film is injected [m]. No film upstream of it.

       **h_liquid_wall** : :class:`python:float` or :func:`python:callable`, :obj:`optional`
           Film-to-wall conductance behind the liquid film [W m⁻² K⁻¹], either a
           constant or ``h(x)``. See the module docstring.











   .. rubric:: Notes

   ``x`` outside the solved film range is clamped to the nearest solved
   station, so a regenerative grid finer than the film grid is fine.
