pyskyfire.ducts.volute¶

Volute geometry generation and one-dimensional loss analysis.

A Volute resolves its own geometry on construction: it sweeps the polar angle from the tongue, and at each station solves the area law for the cross-section size that satisfies it. Volute.solve() then runs Aungier’s meanline loss analysis over the resolved geometry.

Stations follow Aungier’s numbering:

5

Volute inlet, where the swirling flow is collected.

6

Full-collection plane, one revolution from the tongue, where the whole mass flow has been gathered and turns into the exit cone.

7

Exit cone discharge, at the round pipe the volute delivers to.

The analysis is incompressible. Aungier iterates on the full-collection-plane velocity because gas density responds to the losses; for the liquids pyskyfire pumps that feedback is weak, so density is re-evaluated at the mean pressure a few times rather than solved as a thermodynamic loop. Aungier himself notes that density variation through a volute is nearly negligible even for gas.

References¶

Aungier, R. H., Centrifugal Compressors: A Strategy for Aerodynamic Design and Analysis, ASME Press, 2000. Sections 5.7 (Eqs. 5-105 to 5-110) and 11.2 (Eqs. 11-1 to 11-5).

Weber, C. R. and Koronowski, M. E., “Meanline Performance Prediction of Volutes in Centrifugal Compressors,” ASME 86-GT-216, 1986.

Exceptions¶

VoluteConsistencyWarning

Volute inputs are mutually inconsistent or outside a model's range.

Classes¶

Volute

A volute or collector resolved from a section family and an area law.

VoluteGeometry

Resolved passage geometry around the volute.

VoluteResult

Loss breakdown and station conditions for one volute operating point.
